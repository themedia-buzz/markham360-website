Markham Business hosts Getting Your Business Licensed in the City of Markham on Thursday, October 1, 2026, from 2:00 to 4:00 p.m. EDT in the Canada Room at Markham Civic Centre (101 Town Centre Blvd.). Admission is free.

The seminar covers why a municipal business licence matters, licence types (commercial, home-based, and mobile or specialised), the step-by-step application path, and renewal and compliance basics. Official licensing requirements are also summarised on the [City of Markham Business Licence page](https://www.markham.ca/permits-licences-taxes/licences-registrations/business-licence).

Because of construction outside the Main Hall (flag) entrance, that entrance is blocked. Enter the Civic Centre through the Milliken, Thornhill, or Unionville entrances and proceed to the Canada Room.
