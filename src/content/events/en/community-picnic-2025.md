Regional Councillor Alan Ho's Community Picnic 2025 took place on July 19 from 4 to 8 p.m. at Upper Cornell Park (128 Northvale Rd., northwest corner of 16th Avenue and Bur Oak Avenue).

Residents were invited for free food and shows in the park, with games and a lucky draw. The annual picnic is a standing summer gathering meant to bring neighbours together outdoors without a ticket price.
