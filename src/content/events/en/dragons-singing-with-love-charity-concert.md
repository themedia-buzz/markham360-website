In 2024, Regional Councillor Alan Ho co-hosted the Dragons Singing with Love Charity Concert alongside the Canada Fortuna Wishing Well Foundation's James Lee and the Popular Music Club.

The concert surpassed its initial goal and raised $25,000 for the Markham Food Bank. Community partners including Markham Fire & Emergency Service and Foodymart continued their support of the food bank drive tradition that runs alongside these fundraising nights.
