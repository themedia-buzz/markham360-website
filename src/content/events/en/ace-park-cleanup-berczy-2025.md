On Sunday, September 7, 2025, from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m., Active Community Engagement (ACE) Ontario / 政經民社 held a park cleanup at Berczy Park North (447 The Bridle Walk, Markham). Volunteers gathered at 8:45 a.m. so the cleanup could start on time.

The morning was part of ACE’s consecutive Sunday cleanups in Markham parks, giving residents a practical way to care for shared green space together.

Event notice and photos are cross-posted from [ACE Ontario](https://aceontario.org/events).
