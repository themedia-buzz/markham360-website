On Sunday, August 24, 2025, from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m., Active Community Engagement (ACE) Ontario / 政經民社 hosted a volunteer park cleanup at Milliken Mills Park (7600 Kennedy Road, Markham). Volunteers were asked to gather by 8:45 a.m. before the cleanup began.

This outing opened ACE’s late-summer park cleanup series across Markham. Families and neighbours worked side by side to clear litter from paths, courts, and open green space.

Photos and event details are cross-posted from [ACE Ontario](https://aceontario.org/events) so the service day stays visible in Markham 360’s community newspaper.
