On April 19, 2026, from 2 to 5 p.m. at Aaniin Community Centre (5665 14th Ave.), Regional Councillor Alan Ho hosted the Markham Job Fair.

Employers included York Region, Paramedic Services, Fire & Emergency Services, and other local companies with booths on site. The fair is designed as a direct path between residents looking for work and organisations hiring in Markham and York Region.
