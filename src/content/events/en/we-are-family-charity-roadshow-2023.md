On February 19, 2023, the We Are Family Charity Roadshow brought sponsors, charity singers, and volunteers together to support organisations that serve people with disabilities and autism.

Proceeds and awareness supported DD Community Connector and the Chinese Autism Awareness Centre. The roadshow is part of a recurring community fundraising tradition that puts local service organisations in front of residents who want to help.
