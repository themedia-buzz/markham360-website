On July 7, 2024, from 4 to 8 p.m., Regional Councillor Alan Ho hosted the Community Picnic at Aaniin Park.

The afternoon offered free food and shows, games, and a lucky draw in the park. It is the same open invitation as the later 2025 picnic: a summer gathering for residents, without barriers to entry.
