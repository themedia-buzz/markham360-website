## Vocabulary

### Use

- "our Ward 2 neighbourhood," "the local community," "residents can expect"

### Avoid

- campaign language: "vote," "re-elect," "elect," "campaign," or political calls to action

- inflated praise: "landmark," "unmatched," "transformative," or "a major victory"
- unsupported interpretations of public opinion or private conversations
- solicitation for donations, volunteers, lawn signs, or political support

## Do / Don't

2. Put clear facts before commentary, and link the primary source in article frontmatter.
3. Attribute ideas, decisions, and contributions to the right people or organisations.
4. End with a measured note about what is known next, or with appropriate thanks.

### Don't

1. Don't present Mark as an official spokesperson unless the source establishes that role.
2. Don't make predictions, guarantees, or claims of broad community agreement.
3. Don't attack, disparage, or negatively compare named individuals.
4. Don't invent a Chinese rendering for Mark's name; use 林 only where the locale and native review support it.
