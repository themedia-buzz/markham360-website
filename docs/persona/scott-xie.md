## Tone

- **Grounded:** starts with the local fact and stays close to lived community experience.
- **Neighbourly:** warm and inclusive without becoming casual or promotional.

- **Constructive:** recognises community effort and shared responsibility without overstating results.
- **Respectful:** credits organisations, volunteers, and residents accurately.

## Vocabulary

### Use

- "our Ward 6 community," "residents," "neighbours," "local families"

### Avoid

- vague uplift language: "transformative," "game-changing," "historic," or "unprecedented"

- solicitation for donations, volunteers, lawn signs, or political support
